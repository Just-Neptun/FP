\begin{abstract}
    We drive a vibrating reed using alternating current between electrodes on the sides of the metal reed.
    We analyze the resonance spectra and phase shift of the oscillation.
    Firstly, we examine the lowest three harmonics and their respective amplitude peaks.
    We attempt to fit Lorentz curves to the resonances and calculate $Q$-factors of the oscillators.
    After that, we focus on the first harmonic and examine how its frequency changes when the reed is set at different temperatures.
    This ends with a linear fit of the relationship between resonance frequency and temperature, which allows us to calculate a temperature coefficient for the system.
\end{abstract}
