\begin{abstract}
    The determination of quantities of interest is a defining goal of experimental physics.
    This measuring process never produces an exact result.
    Since most measurements happen indirectly, it is crucial to understand how those uncertainties propagate.
    We will implement a way to perform symbolic error propagation in the programming language \verb|Python| using the package \verb|SymPy|.

    After completing a measurement, the experimentalist compares theoretical predictions with the measured data.
    In general, the precise relationship of the measured quantities is not known.
    Assuming a general model dependent on some parameters, the task becomes the estimation of these parameters.
    We implement and examine three methods of estimation: 
    The method of least squares, the maximum-likelihood method, and the method of Bayesian parameter estimation.
\end{abstract}
