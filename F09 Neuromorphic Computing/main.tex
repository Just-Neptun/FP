\section{Lu.i Experiment}

5.2.1 Getting familiar with Lu.i's parameters
Before you begin, connect Lu.i's membrane to the oscilloscope.
• Familiarize yourself with the parameters of the neuron emulation. What happens if you increase the leak potential?
Can you observe the membrane time constant? Note the impact of the individual potentiometers.
• Connect two or multiple neurons to form a simple network and note how signals propagate across neurons, e.g.,
while playing with the synaptic weights and the synaptic time constant.
Pick a configuration where you can approximately measure τref and the threshold ϑ. Take notes of your results and
your methods of determining the individual parameters.
